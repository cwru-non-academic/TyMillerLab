## About

The Ty Miller Lab aims to improve the treatment of brain tumors through the development of innovative immunotherapies.

Our research is driven by a deep understanding of the tumor microenvironment, with a particular focus on the states and functions of myeloid cells within tumors and how they influence the effectiveness of immunotherapies.

This GitHub page serves as a central index for publicly available software, computational workflows, and code associated with our research.
